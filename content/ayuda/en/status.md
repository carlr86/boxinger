The **Status** tab shows the outcome of the board's ideas (hidden ones not included).

## What it shows

- **Key figures:** ideas received, approved, launched, and the average time from proposal to launch.
- **How far do ideas get?:** how many reached each stage (approved, Roadmap, development, launched).
- **How long do ideas take?:** average days from proposed to approved and from approved to launched.
- **Launched by origin:** how many from the Community and how many from the Team were launched.
- **Growth of launched ideas:** how many target each goal and their average impact.
- **Ideas in progress and launched:** the list, with filters.

## Download

Tap **Download** and pick:

- **Excel (CSV):** every number, to work with in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **PDF to share:** a page with the summary, the charts and the list.
