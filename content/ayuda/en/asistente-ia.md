The AI assistant helps the Admin decide better. Open it from the **AI assistant** button on the board.

## Set it up

The first time, tell it what your product is about: what it does, for whom and what its goals are. That gives the AI the context.

## What it does

- **Board ideas:** reads pending and in-review ideas, with their votes and comments, and proposes up to 5 to approve, with the reason and any duplicates.
- **Suggest new ideas:** proposes new ideas for your product, different from the ones you already have. Add the useful ones to the board in one tap; dismiss the rest.

## Privacy and limits

- The AI never gets names or emails: only the ideas' text and their vote and comment counts.
- It includes up to 30 analyses and 20 suggestions per month, shared across all your boards. If you need more, write to us.
