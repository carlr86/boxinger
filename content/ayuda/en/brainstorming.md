The **Brainstorming** board type follows the classic brainstorming flow. Create it from **Create board › Use a template › Brainstorming**.

## 1. Propose and vote

Everyone posts ideas, votes and comments on the board. Anonymous ideas are allowed. In **Settings › Vote scale** you choose how people vote:

| Scale | How each person votes | How it sorts |
|---|---|---|
| Three options | Important, Interesting or Not important | 2, 1 and 0 points |
| 1 to 5 | 1 to 5 stars | Sum of stars |
| Like | A single vote | Number of likes |

> **Important:** The scale can be changed while nobody has voted.

## 2. Group by affinity

In the **Group** tab, voted ideas are on the left. Create groups with **+ Create group** (for example "Onboarding" or "Payments") and drag each idea into its group. Tap a group's name to rename it; deleting a group sends its ideas back to the list.

## 3. Impact and effort

Rate the impact and effort of grouped ideas from their detail. The **Matrix** shows them so you see which ones to do first.

## 4. Accept

Tap **Accept** in the Group view or the Matrix: the idea gets approved and moves to the **Backlog**. That ends the session, with the Backlog full of what you decided to do.
