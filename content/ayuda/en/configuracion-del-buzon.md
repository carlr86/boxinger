Open them from the board's **Settings** tab.

## General

- **Name, description and logo** the Community sees.
- **Visibility:** public, invite-only or private.
- **Who can create ideas:** team members and guests.
- **What guests see:** whether they can see the Roadmap.
- **Vote options:** rename *Important*, *Interesting* and *Not important* to whatever you prefer.
- **Prioritization:** turn **Impact and effort** (the Matrix and Value) and **Growth** on or off. They start on; turning them off deletes nothing.
- **Anonymous ideas:** allow ideas to be posted anonymously or not; only Admins see who proposed them. Starts on.

## Categories

Create, rename and sort the categories used to classify ideas.

## Community

Invitations, guests, access by domain and access requests.

## Integrations

Zapier, Make, Google Forms and [Slack notices](/app/ayuda/avisos-en-slack).

## Empty the board

In the **Danger zone** you can delete every idea on the board, with its votes and comments. Settings, categories, members, guests and the link stay. To confirm, type the board's name.

> **Important:** Emptying can't be undone. Download a copy with **Export** first.

## Delete the board

From **My boards**, in the board's menu, **Delete board** removes it completely, with all its ideas.
