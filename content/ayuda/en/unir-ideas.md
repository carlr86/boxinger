When two people ask for the same thing in different words, the team can merge those ideas into one.

## Step by step

1. Open the duplicate idea (the one that will go away).
2. In the **⋯** menu, tap **Merge with another idea**.
3. Search for and pick the main idea, and confirm.

## What happens when merging

- The duplicate's **votes** move to the main idea. If someone voted for both, it counts once.
- Whoever proposed the duplicate adds an **Important** vote to the main idea.
- **Comments** move to the main idea, marked with where they came from.
- If the main idea had no image, it takes the duplicate's.
- The main idea shows **Also requested as**, with the original title.
- People who took part in the duplicate get a notice in the bell.
- The old link leads to the main idea.

> **Important:** Merging can't be undone.
