## Rate an idea

Open the idea and, under **Impact and effort**, pick from 1 to 5 how much impact it would have and how much effort it would take. The rating shows on the card for the team, never for the Community.

## The Matrix

The **Matrix** tab places approved, rated ideas in four zones:

| Zone | What it is |
|---|---|
| Quick wins | High impact, low effort: start here. |
| Big bets | High impact, but they take a lot of effort. |
| Minor improvements | Low effort and low impact. |
| Review | A lot of effort for the expected impact. |

The size of each bubble shows the impact. On the right is the list sorted by priority.

## Value on the Roadmap

On the Roadmap, each idea shows its **Value = Impact × Vote score ÷ Effort**. The higher, the more value for the effort.

> **Tip:** If your team doesn't use these ratings, the Admin can turn them off in **Settings › Type and modules**: the Matrix, the card rows and the Value disappear. Nothing already entered is deleted.
