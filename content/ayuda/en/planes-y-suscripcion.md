## The plans

| | Free | Pro | Team | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | USD 9.99 / month | USD 19.99 / month | Custom |
| Teams and boards | 1 and 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Members per team | Just you | Up to 4 | Up to 20 | As agreed |
| Matrix, Roadmap, Status, Updates | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Import, merge ideas, images | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Slack and integrations | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| AI assistant | No | No | Yes | Yes, with more usage |

## Pay

From **My profile › Subscription**, pick the plan and the payment method:

- **Mercado Pago**, for payments in Argentina, in pesos.
- **International card** (Creem), in dollars, from any country.

The plan turns on within seconds, once the payment is confirmed. The subscription renews every month.

## Enterprise

For more members or more AI usage, tap **Contact us** on the Enterprise card and we'll put together a proposal.

## Cancel

In **My profile › Subscription**, tap **Cancel subscription**. No more charges, and you keep your plan until the end of the paid period. Then the account goes to Free:

- nothing is deleted;
- you choose which board stays active, and the rest become read-only;
- if you come back to your plan, you get everything back as it was.

> **Tip:** Before the period ends you can tap **Back to Pro** or **Back to Team** and the subscription continues without paying again.
