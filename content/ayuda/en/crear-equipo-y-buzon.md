## Create your account

1. Go to Boxinger and tap **Create a free account**.
2. Sign up with your email and a password, or with Google.
3. If you used email, confirm it with the link we send you.

## Your team and your first board

The first time you sign in, Boxinger asks for your **team's name** (for example, your company or product) and your **first board's** name, and whether it's public or invite-only.

A **team** groups the people who decide. A **board** is where ideas for a product or a topic arrive. A team can have several boards.

## Create more boards or teams

From **My boards**, tap **Create board** inside a team, or **Create team**.

> **Important:** On the Free plan you get 1 team with 1 board. With Pro, Team or Enterprise they're unlimited.
