## The bell

At the top right, the bell gathers the app's notices: comments, status changes, new ideas to review, launches and more. Tap a notice to go to the idea.

## Emails

Boxinger emails you, among other things, when:

- someone comments on your idea;
- your idea changes status (approved or rejected, with the reason);
- an idea you proposed or voted for enters the Roadmap or launches;
- you're invited to a board or a team.

The team also gets a **daily digest** with the new comments on its boards.

## Choose what you get

Go to **My profile › Notifications** and turn off the emails you don't want. Emails needed for the service (security, payments, invitations) are still sent.

> **Tip:** For your team to hear about it in Slack, see [Slack notices](/app/ayuda/avisos-en-slack).
