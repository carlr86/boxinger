Both buttons are at the top right of the board: **Import** (down arrow) and **Export** (up arrow).

## Import ideas

Use it to bring in feedback you already have in spreadsheets, surveys or your support system.

1. Tap **Import** and upload a **CSV or Excel** file. You can download a sample template.
2. Point out which column is the title, description, date, category and who asked for it.
3. Check the preview and confirm.

Ideas come in with the *Imported* tag and their original date, ready to vote on. Only the team sees who asked for each one. Votes in the file aren't imported.

> **Tip:** If you made a mistake, you can undo a whole import from the same window.

## Export ideas

Tap **Export** to download a CSV file with **every idea** on the board: status, votes, comments, impact, effort, Roadmap, growth, author and link. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
