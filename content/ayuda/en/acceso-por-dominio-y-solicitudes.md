## Access by email domain

On an invite-only board, you can let in anyone with an email from a certain domain, for example `@mycompany.com`, without inviting them one by one.

1. Go to **Board settings › Community**.
2. Add one or more domains.

## Access requests

If someone opens the link of an invite-only board, they can **request access**. The Admin gets a notice, and in **Settings › Community** can accept or decline each request. The person gets an email when accepted.
