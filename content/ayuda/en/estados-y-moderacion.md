## An idea's statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Pending review | Just posted, nobody looked at it yet. |
| In review | The team is analyzing it. |
| Approved | It will be done: it goes to the Backlog and can move to the Roadmap. |
| Rejected | It won't be done, with the reason visible. |

To change the status, open the idea and use the status buttons. When **rejecting**, Boxinger asks for a reason: the Community sees it and whoever proposed the idea gets it by email.

## Hide an idea

From the idea's **⋯** menu, **Hide from the Community** takes it out of guests' view (spam, sensitive data, something off-topic). The team still sees it and can show it again.

## Ideas that arrive through integrations

Ideas from Zapier, Make, Google Forms or Slack arrive **hidden**, with the *Via integration* tag, and show in the board's **To review** filter. There you can:

- **Publish:** the Community sees it.
- **Reject:** with a reason; it stays hidden.
- **Archive:** it leaves *To review* and goes to **Archived**. Publishing it un-archives it.

## Edit and delete

The team can edit the title, description and category, and delete an idea from the **⋯** menu. Deleting can't be undone.

> **Tip:** If two ideas ask for the same thing, don't delete one: [merge them](/app/ayuda/unir-ideas) and no votes are lost.
