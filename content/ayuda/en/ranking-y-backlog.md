## Ranking

Shows the **10 ideas with the highest score** among those pending or in review. The score adds 2 for each *Important* vote and 1 for each *Interesting*. It's the fastest way to see what your community wants most.

## Backlog

Gathers the team's **approved ideas**, newest first, with the approval date and the final votes. They're the candidates for the [Roadmap](/app/ayuda/roadmap).

> **Tip:** To prioritize within the Backlog, rate impact and effort and look at the [Matrix](/app/ayuda/impacto-esfuerzo-y-matriz).
