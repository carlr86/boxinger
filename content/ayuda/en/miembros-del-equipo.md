Members are the people on your team who review, prioritize and plan ideas. Everything is handled from **My boards**, in the team's menu, with **Manage team**.

## Invite a member

1. Open **Manage team** (or **Invite members to the team**).
2. Type one or more emails.
3. Choose whether they access **every board** on the team or just one.
4. Tap **Invite**: they get an invitation by email.

## Make a member an Admin

On Team and Enterprise, the **Admin Owner** (the account owner) can give the Admin role to any member:

1. In **Manage team**, find the person in the list.
2. Tap **Make Admin** and confirm.

To take the role away, tap **Remove Admin**: they go back to Member.

An Admin can set up boards, invite and remove members, import and export, connect integrations and see who posted anonymous ideas. They can't touch the subscription, delete the team or remove other Admins.

> **Important:** The Admin Owner gets an email whenever an Admin deletes or empties a board, exports ideas or connects an integration. If the account leaves Team or Enterprise, Admins go back to members.

## Limits per plan

| Plan | People per team (members and Admins) |
|---|---|
| Free | Just you |
| Pro | Up to 4 (no Admins) |
| Team | Up to 20 |
| Enterprise | As many as you agree on |

## Board access and removing people

- In **Board access**, tap a member's name to remove or restore their access to a board. Admins always see all of them.
- **Remove** takes someone off the team. Only the Admin Owner can remove an Admin.
