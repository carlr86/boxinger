Members are the people on your team who review, prioritize and plan ideas.

## Invite a member

1. In **My boards**, open the team's menu and tap **Manage team** (or **Invite members to the team**).
2. Type the email and pick their role: **Member** or **Admin**.
3. Choose whether they access **every board** on the team or only some.
4. They get an invitation by email.

## Limits per plan

| Plan | Members per team |
|---|---|
| Free | Just you |
| Pro | Up to 4 |
| Team | Up to 20 |
| Enterprise | As many as you agree on |

> **Tip:** From the same place you can change each member's access, make them Admin or remove them from the team.
