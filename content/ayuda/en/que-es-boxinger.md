Boxinger gathers in one place the ideas that today arrive scattered: by email, by chat, in meetings or in spreadsheets. Every idea can be voted on and commented on, and the team reviews it, prioritizes it and lets people know when it launches.

## How it works, in 4 steps

1. **Ideas come in.** Your community (customers, users, employees) or your team proposes them. They can also arrive on their own from other tools.
2. **People vote and comment.** Each person marks whether the idea is *Important*, *Interesting* or *Not important*. That builds the Ranking.
3. **The team decides.** It reviews each idea, approves it or rejects it with a reason, and plans the approved ones in the Roadmap.
4. **Everyone hears about it, and you measure it.** When an idea enters the Roadmap or launches, Boxinger tells whoever proposed or voted for it. Status shows what happened to every idea.

## The parts of a board

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Board | Every idea, with search and filters. |
| Ranking | The 10 pending ideas with the highest score. |
| Backlog | Approved ideas, candidates for the Roadmap. |
| Matrix | Impact versus effort, to prioritize (Team). |
| Roadmap | What's happening now, next and later (Team). |
| Status | Metrics on what happened to the ideas (Team). |
| Updates | What the team already launched. |
| Settings | Name, categories, community and integrations (Team). |
