Boxinger can post in one of your team's Slack channels whenever something happens on the board. Each message links to the idea.

## Connect

1. Go to **Board settings › Integrations**.
2. Under **Slack notices**, tap **Connect to Slack**.
3. Slack opens: pick your workspace and the channel, and tap **Allow**.
4. A first message arrives in the channel confirming the connection.

Boxinger can only post in that channel: it doesn't read messages or any other Slack data.

## What to post

Each notice can be turned on or off:

- **New idea**
- **Idea to review** (the ones arriving through integrations)
- **Enters the Roadmap**
- **Idea launched**
- **Weekly summary**, on Mondays

You can also **Send a test message**, **Change channel** or **Disconnect**.

> **Important:** If your Slack workspace requires approval for new apps, Slack lets you send the request to your admin from the same screen. If the channel is later deleted or the app removed, notices pause and the screen asks you to connect again.
