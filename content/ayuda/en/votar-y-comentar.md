## Vote

On any idea, tap **Vote** and pick an option:

- **Important:** I need it.
- **Interesting:** it would be nice.
- **Not important:** it doesn't affect me.

You can change your vote anytime. You can't vote for your own idea, and once the idea is approved or rejected, voting closes.

An idea's **score** adds 2 points for each *Important* and 1 for each *Interesting*. That score builds the [Ranking](/app/ayuda/ranking-y-backlog).

> **Tip:** The Admin can rename the three vote options in the board settings, so they speak your community's language.

## Comment

Open the idea and write your comment at the bottom. You can edit or delete it. Comments can get a *like* or a *dislike*.

- The **Team** can reply to a comment: the reply shows below it, highlighted.
- The Team can **hide** a comment that doesn't belong.
- Whoever proposed the idea gets an email for each new comment.
