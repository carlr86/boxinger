## Step by step

1. On the board, tap **+ New idea**.
2. Write a **title** that sums up the idea in one line (5 to 80 characters).
3. In the **description**, explain what problem it solves and for whom (20 to 2,000 characters).
4. Pick a **category**.
5. If you want, **attach an image** and check **Post anonymously**.
6. Tap **Post idea**.

The idea is posted right away with the **Pending review** status and the **Community** or **Team** tag, depending on who posted it.

## Attach an image

A screenshot or a photo helps explain the idea. **One image per idea**, in JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB: Boxinger shrinks it before uploading and removes the photo's hidden data, such as its location.

> **Important:** Images are on the Pro, Team and Enterprise plans. On a private or invite-only board, only those who can see the board see the image.

## Post anonymously

If you check **Post anonymously**, the idea shows as "Anonymous" to the Community and to team members. **Only the team's Admins** know it's yours. You still get emails about comments, status changes and your idea's launch.

- Your comments on that idea also show as "Anonymous (idea author)".
- You can show your name later: open the idea and tap **Show my name**.
- If you don't see the option, the board's Admin turned it off in the settings.

## Edit your idea

You can correct your idea whenever you want, even if it already has votes or is approved: open it and tap **Edit my idea**. You change the title, description, category and image. Other people's ideas can only be edited by the team.

## Who can post ideas?

The Admin decides whether **members** and **guests** can post ideas, in **Settings › Who can create ideas**. If you can't, you can still vote and comment.

> **Tip:** Before posting, search the board: if the idea already exists, vote for it. That counts more than two similar ideas.
