Two optional pieces of data to prioritize better:

- **MRR:** how much MRR (monthly recurring revenue) the customers asking for the idea represent. You enter it by hand, in the board's currency.
- **Customer segments:** which kind of customers ask for it, for example Startup, Small Business or BPO. An idea can have several.

Only the **Team** sees them. The Community and guests never do.

## Turning them on

1. Go to **Settings › General › Type and modules**.
2. Turn on **MRR**, **Customer segments** or both. They start off.

Further down, the **MRR and customer segments** section shows up:

- **MRR currency:** USD, ARS, EUR, BRL, MXN, CLP, COP, UYU, PEN or GBP. It's the same for every idea on the board.
- **Customer segments:** the list starts with Startup, Small business, Mid-size company, Large company and BPO. You can add, rename and delete them, up to 12.
- **Show on the cards:** when on, the MRR and segments also show on the cards of the Board, the Roadmap and the Matrix.

They're available on Digital product and blank boards. Brainstorming doesn't have them.

## Filling them in

Open the idea and, in the **Customers** section:

- Type the **MRR** and leave the field to save it. To remove it, leave it empty.
- Tap the **segments** that apply. You can pick several.

## Good to know

- Deleting a segment takes it off the ideas that had it.
- Changing the currency doesn't convert the amounts: only the currency shown changes.
- When you [merge ideas](/app/ayuda/unir-ideas), the main one keeps its MRR (if it had none, it takes the duplicate's) and gets the segments of both.
- **Export** adds the MRR, Currency and Segments columns.

> **Tip:** Turning the modules off doesn't delete anything. Turn them back on and everything is as it was.
