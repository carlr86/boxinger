**Updates** shows what the team already launched, newest first and grouped by month. Every idea marked as launched shows up on its own: there's nothing to write.

- Everyone who can see the board sees it: team and guests.
- Each update shows who proposed it (or "Anonymous"), the category and the votes.
- When an idea launches, the whole board community gets a notice in the bell, and whoever proposed or voted for it gets an email.

## Download

Tap **Download** to get it as **Excel (CSV)** or **PDF to share**, for example to send to your customers.
