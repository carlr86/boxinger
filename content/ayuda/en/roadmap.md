The Roadmap sorts approved ideas into three columns: **Now**, **Next** and **Later**. You can rename each column.

## Move an idea to the Roadmap

- Drag an idea from the Backlog to a column, or
- open the idea and pick the column.

The first time an idea enters the Roadmap, Boxinger tells whoever proposed or voted for it, by email and in the bell. Moving it between columns doesn't repeat the notice.

## Each card

- **Priority:** High, Medium or Low.
- **Development:** Not started, In progress or Launched.
- **Design** and **PRD / SPEC:** checkboxes for what's ready.
- **Value:** impact × score ÷ effort (if you use impact and effort).

You can see cards **Expanded** or **Compact**.

## Launch an idea

From the card's menu, **Mark as launched**. The idea moves to the *Launched* column, shows in [Updates](/app/ayuda/novedades), and Boxinger tells whoever proposed or voted for it, plus the whole board community in the bell.

> **Tip:** The Admin can show the Roadmap to the Community in **Settings › What guests see**. They see the columns and each idea's stage, but not priority, impact or effort.
