Boxinger has two big groups: the **Team**, which decides, and the **Community**, which proposes and votes.

## Team

| Role | Who | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin Owner** | The account owner (just one) | Everything: the subscription and payments, deleting the team, making others Admin and removing the role, and everything an Admin does. |
| **Admin** | Team people the Admin Owner gave the role to (Team and Enterprise) | Set up boards, invite and remove members, import and export, connect integrations and see who posted anonymous ideas. They don't touch the subscription, don't delete the team and can't remove other Admins. |
| **Member** | The rest of the team | Review, prioritize and plan ideas on the boards they can access. They can post ideas if the Admin allows it. |

Whoever creates a board can also manage it, even as a Member.

## Community

- **Guests:** join a board with the link or an invitation. They propose ideas (if the board allows it), edit their own, vote and comment.
- **Visitors:** on a public board, anyone with the link can see the ideas. To vote or comment they need to sign in.

## What each one sees

| | Community | Members | Admins |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board, Ranking, Backlog, Updates | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Roadmap | If the Admin allows it | Yes | Yes |
| Matrix and Status | No | Yes | Yes |
| Board settings | No | Partly | Yes |
| Author of an anonymous idea | No | No | Yes |
| Subscription and payments | No | No | Only the Admin Owner |

> **Tip:** The Free plan is just for you. With Pro you add up to 4 people per team, with Team up to 20 (members and Admins together), and with Enterprise as many as you agree on. More in [Team members and Admins](/app/ayuda/miembros-del-equipo).
