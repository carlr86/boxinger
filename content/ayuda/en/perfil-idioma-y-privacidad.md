Open **My profile** from the menu with your name, at the top right.

## Your details

Change your name and photo. If you sign in with Google, Boxinger uses your Google account's photo.

## Language

In the menu with your name, pick **Español** or **English**. It changes the whole interface and the emails we send you.

## Notifications

In **My profile › Notifications** you choose which emails to get. More in [Notices and notifications](/app/ayuda/avisos-y-notificaciones).

## Cookies on the public site

On Boxinger's public pages (home, Slack and legal texts) a cookie notice shows two buttons, **Reject** and **Accept**. If you accept, we use Microsoft Clarity to see how those pages are browsed and improve them. If you reject, it isn't loaded. It's never used inside the app, so your boards and ideas aren't recorded.

To change your answer, click **Cookie preferences** at the bottom of those pages. Your answer is remembered for a year in that browser.

## Delete your account

In **My profile** you can delete your account. Your profile and sign-in data are removed. Ideas and comments you left on other teams' boards stay as from a "Deleted user". If you own an account, your teams and boards are deleted with all their content.

> **Important:** Deleting the account can't be undone. If you have a subscription, cancel it first.

More in the [privacy policy](https://boxinger.com/privacidad).
