Each idea can target one or more growth goals:

- **Acquisition:** bring in new users or customers.
- **Activation:** get them to start using the product and find value in it.
- **Retention:** get them to stay and come back.
- **Monetization:** get them to pay, or pay more.
- **Prevent churn:** keep them from leaving.

## How to mark it

Open the idea and, in the **Growth** section, tap the goals that apply. They show on the card for the team.

## Where it's used

In [Status](/app/ayuda/status), the **Growth of launched ideas** card shows how many launched ideas target each goal and their average impact.

> **Tip:** If you don't use it, the Admin can turn growth off in **Settings › Type and modules**. Nothing already entered is deleted.
