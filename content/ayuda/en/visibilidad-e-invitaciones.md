## Kinds of board

| Visibility | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Public | Anyone with the link can see the ideas. To vote or comment, they sign in and join. |
| Invite-only | Only the people you invited or approved. |
| Private | Only the team, no Community. |

Change it in **Board settings › Visibility**. The board's URL doesn't change.

## Invite your community

- **With the link:** copy the board link from **My boards** or the settings and share it.
- **By email:** in **Settings › Community**, type the emails and tap **Invite**. They get an email with the link.

In **Settings › Community** you see your guests and can block someone if needed.

> **Tip:** To let your whole team or a company's customers in directly, use [access by domain](/app/ayuda/acceso-por-dominio-y-solicitudes).
